LINK

LIKED TRAPPED SHARED

DESCRIPTION

Ailanthus altissima, known as the Tree of Heaven, is a species native to China and introduced in Europe in the 18th century. While appreciated for its extraordinary resilience, it is also feared for its highly invasive nature. "Tree of Heaven or Hell?" explores this debate by analyzing 76 YouTube videos and 925 comments, revealing how public discussions around Ailanthus reflect broader power dynamics between humans and nature. The project guides the user through three artifacts: a supercut of videos, a scrolling LED display that visualizes comment polarization, and a catalog of eradication methods.

ROLE

Editorial design, concept development, typography, experimentation, translation of digital culture into print.

DESCRIPTION

A special edition of Italy's most popular magazine, Settimana Enigmistica, entirely focused on ASCII Art. Every page element is constructed from text characters, bridging the digital art movement with a beloved analogue publication. The combination of two opposite worlds, digital and tangible, creates a unique medium for spreading knowledge about a little-known artistic tradition.

LINK

SINESTESIE.IT

DESCRIPTION

A research-based project focused on accessibility in contemporary art, developed through the analysis of three artworks from the Villa Panza collection in Varese. Through an accessible video and a series of handcrafted tactile artefacts, the project translates visual artworks into multisensory experiences designed for blind and visually impaired audiences. The project explored how artistic languages can be interpreted and communicated beyond sight, combining visual communication, material experimentation and inclusive design principles.
